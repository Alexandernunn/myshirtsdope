name: Shopping entry path
description: User-stated acquisition path that determines where product discovery should start.
---

Visitors typically arrive on the product page from Facebook ads.

**Why:** The user stated that this is the typical entry path, so homepage-first discovery improvements would miss many incoming shoppers.

**How to apply:** Prioritize relevant discovery on product landing pages while keeping the advertised item and purchase controls primary. Do not assume visitors have browsed the homepage.
